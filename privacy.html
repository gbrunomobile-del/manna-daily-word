Privacy Policy

Manna: Daily Word · Last updated 31 August 2026

Manna: Daily Word (“Manna”, “the app”) is made by Giancarlo Bruno. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. It is short because the app does very little with it.

1. We do not collect your data

Manna has no user accounts on our servers and no analytics service. Everything the app records about you — your reading progress, which chapters you have gathered, your streak, your progress through The Way — is stored only on your device. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

If you delete the app, that information is deleted with it. We hold no copy and cannot restore it.

2. Sign in with Apple

If you choose to sign in with Apple, Apple provides the app with an anonymous identifier, and — only the first time you authorise it — the name and email address you elect to share. Apple allows you to hide your real email address, and the app works normally if you do.

This information is stored on your device only. It is not sent to us.

3. Scripture text

Bible passages are fetched as you read them from bible-api.com, which serves the World English Bible, a public domain translation. These requests contain the passage being requested and nothing identifying you. We do not control that service; see its own terms for how it handles requests.

4. Children

Manna is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

5. Changes to this policy

If a future version of Manna introduces accounts that sync your progress between devices, that would involve storing your information on a server, and this policy will be updated before that version is released. The date at the top of this page will change when it does.
