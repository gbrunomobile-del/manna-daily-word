Common questions

Which translation does Manna use?

The World English Bible, a modern public domain translation. It is free to read and share without licensing restrictions.

Do I need an account?

No. Manna works without one, and your progress is kept on your device.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

At present, yes — progress is stored on the device rather than on a server. Syncing between devices is planned.

I missed a few days. Have I lost my streak?

Nothing is taken away for being absent. The count simply starts again, and the app will say welcome back rather than anything else. The reading plan waits where you left it.

Why does the reading plan start at day one?

The year begins the day you begin, not on the first of January, so everyone starts at Genesis whenever they join.

What are the illustrations?

Engravings in the manner of Gustave Doré, whose Bible illustrations have been in the public domain since long before this app existed.
